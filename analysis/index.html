  2. Long-form

    Tracking Canada's Export Diversification ↗

    Despite exporting goods and services to over 200 countries and territories, Canada’s export portfolio behaves as though it trades with just two equally sized partners. This paradox is captured by Statistics Canada’s export market concentration index, a measure of export dependence on a few trading partners. The index ranges from 0 (perfect diversification) to 1 (absolute dependence on a single partner).

    May 27, 2026 · on Substack
